About La Salette of Aurora, Inc.

A School of Excellence since 1952 · Brgy. Sta. Rosa, Aurora, Isabela, Philippines

📖Our History

La Salette of Aurora, Inc., one among the La Salette schools in the province of Isabela, is located in Brgy. Sta. Rosa, Aurora — a town west of Cabatuan across the Magat River. In 1952, the late Rev. Fr. Paul Doulliard, MS, Parish Priest of St. Isidore Parish, envisioned a school for the Catholic families' children of his parish. By March 1952, the school was incorporated as a non-stock, non-profit institution for secondary education, opening its doors on July 1, 1952 with 193 students. Since then, it has served the educational needs of Western Isabela — Aurora, Burgos, and San Manuel — earning recognition from the Ministry of Education and Culture. Today, it is a PAASCU-accredited Basic Education institution (Level I, since 2016).

🎯Vision & Mission

Vision: La Salette School System is a community of Catholic schools called to evangelize the young in the spirit of Our Lady of La Salette's message of Reconciliation.

Mission: To provide excellent Catholic education, to enable the young to attain "fullness of life." (John 10:10)
